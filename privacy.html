Privacy Policy

Spend Dekho

Last updated: July 2026

Spend Dekho does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Data We Collect

None. Spend Dekho has no backend server, no cloud sync, no user accounts, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. The app does not have network access to send your financial data anywhere.

Where Your Data Lives

All information you enter — your name, income sources, expenses, categories, and savings goals — is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. It never leaves your phone, and we (the developer) never see it.

Notifications

Spend Dekho uses local, on-device notifications (such as your daily spend allowance reminder) to help you stay on budget. These are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external push notification service or server.

Data You Export

If you use the PDF export feature, the file is generated and saved directly on your device. You control where it goes from there (e.g., sharing it via Messages, Mail, or saving it to Files) — we have no visibility into or access to exported files.

Deleting Your Data

Since all data is stored only on your device, uninstalling the app permanently deletes all your Spend Dekho data. There is no account to close and no server-side copy to remove.

Children's Privacy

Spend Dekho does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud backup feature), this page will be updated accordingly, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
