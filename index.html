Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add an expense or income entry? Tap the + button on the Dashboard, or use the Expenses / Income tab, to add a new entry. You'll need at least one income source added before you can log expenses.
Can I edit or delete a past entry? Yes — swipe left on any expense in the current month to reveal Edit and Delete options. Past months are read-only to keep your historical records accurate.
How is my "Daily Spend Allowance" calculated? It's based on your remaining budget for the month (income minus expenses minus your savings goal), divided evenly across the days left in the month.
Where is my data stored? Entirely on your device. Spend Dekho does not use the cloud, does not require an account, and does not share your data with anyone. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I export my data? Yes — use the export option in the app to generate a PDF summary of any month's income, expenses, and savings.
